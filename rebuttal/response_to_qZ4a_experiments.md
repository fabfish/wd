**Setup.** CIFAR-100 (50K train / 10K test), 3 architectures: ResNet-18, VGG-16 (no residual), ResNet-50; 100 epochs, CosineAnnealingLR, 2 random seeds (42, 123). Three experiment grids: Exp 1 — 3 optimizer variants (SGD, SGD+WD, SGDM+WD) $\times$ 8 LRs = 24 configs; Exp 2 — SGDM, 5 LRs $\times$ 7 $\lambda$ values = 35 configs; Exp 3 — SGDM, 4 batch sizes with linear LR rule $\eta=0.1\times(B/128)$ $\times$ 6 $\lambda$ values = 24 configs.

**Observations.** (1) *Stability boundary*: SGD and SGDM+WD peak at $\eta^*=0.1$ on all 3 architectures; SGD+WD peaks at $\eta^*\in[0.5,1.0]$, confirming the ordering SGD+WD (widest) > SGD > SGDM+WD (tightest). (2) *$\eta$–$\lambda$ interaction*: clear anti-diagonal heatmap pattern across all architectures. (3) *Batch scaling*: under the linear LR rule, $\lambda^*\in[5\times10^{-4},10^{-3}]$ holds for all batch sizes and all architectures. Additionally, extending to 7 $\lambda$ values over $\eta\in[0.0002,5.0]$ shows that the optimal $\eta\times\lambda$ concentrates near $10^{-4}$, consistent with $\lambda\eta\approx\text{const}$.

**Conclusion.** Across 3 architecturally diverse models (residual vs. non-residual, shallow vs. deep) and independent runs, all three theoretical predictions are consistently confirmed: (1) the stability boundary ordering SGD+WD > SGD > SGDM+WD, (2) the inverse $\eta$–$\lambda$ relationship, and (3) the batch size–weight decay coupling under linear LR scaling. Results are robust to random seed variation (half-range <0.5% in stable regions) and generalize beyond a single architecture. New figures and tables are available at our anonymous repository.
