**Setup.** We added CIFAR-100 experiments on ResNet-18, VGG-16, and ResNet-50. All models were trained for 100 epochs with CosineAnnealingLR and AMP. We report mean $\pm$ half-range over 2 seeds (42, 123), plus 4 ResNet-18 reruns. The study includes: Exp 1, SGD / SGD+WD / SGDM+WD over 8 learning rates; Exp 2, SGDM over 5 learning rates and 7 weight-decay values at $B=128$; Exp 3, SGDM over batch sizes $64,128,256,512$ with $\eta=0.1\times(B/128)$ and 6 weight-decay values. 

**Observations.** In Exp 1, all three architectures show the same stability-boundary ordering: SGD+WD is widest, SGD is intermediate, and SGDM+WD is tightest. In Exp 2, the $\eta$-$\lambda$ heatmaps show a clear anti-diagonal pattern across all architectures: larger $\eta$ consistently prefers smaller $\lambda^*$. In Exp 3, under linear LR scaling, the optimal $\lambda^*$ stays in $[5\times10^{-4},10^{-3}]$ across batch sizes and architectures. We also extended Exp 2 with test loss and a wider grid, the best $\eta\lambda$ lies near $10^{-4}$ for 5 of 7 $\lambda$ values.

**Conclusion.** These experiments support all three predictions across residual/non-residual and shallow/deep architectures: the stability-boundary ordering, the inverse $\eta$-$\lambda$ relation, and the batch-size/weight-decay coupling. In the stable region, the half-range is typically below 0.5%.
